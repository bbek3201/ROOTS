# Landing photographs

Anything dropped in here (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`) becomes the
photography on the public landing page, in filename order:

1. the first file is the full-bleed hero
2. the next three fill the three plates further down the page

They are served publicly, so put nothing here that belongs to a family's
private archive. With the folder empty the landing page composes itself out of
the warm washes instead — it is finished either way.
