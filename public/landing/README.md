# Landing photographs

Anything dropped in here (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`) becomes the
photography on the public landing page, in filename order:

1. the first file is the full-bleed hero
2. the next three fill the three plates further down the page

The first file is also the window at the foot of the signed-in sidebar, so
whatever goes in slot 1 is seen on every page of the product, not only by
strangers on the front door.

They are served publicly, so put nothing here that belongs to a family's
private archive. With the folder empty every one of those surfaces composes
itself out of the warm washes instead — it is finished either way.

## What is in here now

Four landscapes, **drawn rather than photographed**: dawn, summer pasture,
autumn light and winter dusk over a steppe horizon. They were generated from
layered gradients so the product could be looked at, and reviewed, with real
raster images in every slot — a gradient placeholder reads as a missing image
at hero size, and a page that has only ever been seen empty has not been seen.

They are stand-ins. Replace them with real photographs the moment there are
photographs to use; nothing in the code knows or cares which is which.
